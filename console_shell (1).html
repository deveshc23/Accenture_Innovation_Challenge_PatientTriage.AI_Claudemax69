Waiting room

Live ranking within colour. The timer re-scores every waiting patient; two triggers are mandatory — a safe-wait breach, or re-recorded vitals that are worse. Every change in rank is traced: the ▲/▼ beside a patient's number shows how far they moved since the last render, hover it for why, and the Queue movement panel and audit chain carry the same explanation.

Demonstration cohort, deliberately enriched. Ten of these thirty arrivals are true ESI-2 against a real-world base rate of roughly 10–19%. A cohort that mirrored real prevalence would put four hard cases in fifty and prove nothing in a six-minute demo. The engine is not over-triaging — the room is stacked on purpose, and the ground truth for every patient was assigned before the engine ever saw them.
